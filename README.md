# 📦 Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Data Analysis & BI Portfolio Project

An end-to-end exploratory data analysis and business intelligence project using 100,000+ real orders from the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset (2016–2018). This project investigates revenue drivers, geographic performance, product category trends, and the operational impact of delivery latency on customer satisfaction.

---

## 🚀 Key Business Findings

### 1. Revenue & Geographic Performance
* **Top Revenue State:** **São Paulo (SP)** dominates total sales revenue by a massive margin, acting as the core economic hub for the platform.
* **Monthly Trends:** Sales grew steadily through 2017 with significant seasonal spikes, highlighting key promotional windows.

### 2. Top Product Categories
* **Health & Beauty** (`health_beauty`) ranks as the highest-grossing product category in terms of total revenue, closely followed by *Watches & Gifts* and *Bed, Bath & Table*.

### 3. Impact of Delivery Latency on Customer Satisfaction
* **On-Time Deliveries:** Orders delivered on or before the estimated date achieve a solid average rating of **4.15 / 5 stars**.
* **Late Deliveries:** When shipments run late, customer satisfaction crashes down to an average of **2.26 / 5 stars**—nearly a 2-star drop. Punctuality is the single largest controllable driver of customer retention.

## 💡 Recommendations for Olist Operations
1. **Fulfill & Dispatch Bottlenecks:** Partner more closely with regional logistics providers in underperforming states to curb late deliveries and protect review scores.
2. **Inventory Focus:** Double down on marketing and seller acquisition for high-margin categories like *Health & Beauty* and *Watches & Gifts*.
